# HR Analytics: Employee Attrition & Flight Risk Scoring

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI**.

The main purpose of this dashboard is to help HR teams and business managers understand **employee attrition patterns** and identify employees who may have a **higher risk of leaving the organization**.

The dashboard converts employee data into meaningful HR insights that can support better employee retention and workforce planning decisions.



## 🎯 Project Objectives

The key objectives of this project are:

- Analyze overall employee attrition.
- Calculate and monitor the employee attrition rate.
- Identify departments with higher employee attrition.
- Analyze attrition across different age groups.
- Understand the relationship between salary and employee attrition.
- Analyze attrition based on employee experience.
- Identify employees with different levels of flight risk.
- Identify departments and job roles with higher flight risk.
- Provide actionable insights for HR decision-making.



## 📊 Dashboard Pages

The Power BI report consists of three interactive pages.

### 1. HR Overview

This page provides a high-level summary of the organization's workforce.

It includes:

- Total Employees
- Active Employees
- Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate
- Average Age
- Average Experience
- Attrition by Department
- Attrition by Age Group
- Attrition by Salary Slab
- Attrition by Experience
- Age Group Slicer

This page helps users quickly understand the overall HR situation.


### 2. Employee Attrition Analysis

This page focuses specifically on employee attrition patterns.

It helps analyze:

- Which departments have higher attrition
- Which age groups show higher attrition
- Which salary ranges have higher attrition
- How employee experience relates to attrition
- Department-wise attrition trends
- Other employee-level factors associated with leaving the organization

These insights can help HR identify areas where employee retention strategies may be required.



### 3. Employee Flight Risk Analysis

This page focuses on identifying employees according to their potential flight risk.

It includes:

- High Risk Employees
- Medium Risk Employees
- Low Risk Employees
- Average Flight Risk Score
- High-Risk Employees by Department
- High-Risk Employees by Job Role
- Flight Risk Distribution
- Employee Flight Risk Details

The dashboard categorizes employees into different risk levels to help HR prioritize retention efforts.



## 📈 Key Insights

Some important insights identified through the dashboard include:

- The organization has **1,417 employees**.
- The overall employee attrition rate is approximately **16.73%**.
- The **Administrative department** has a high number of employees experiencing attrition.
- Employees in the **6–10 LPA salary range** show comparatively higher attrition.
- Employees in the earlier stages of their careers show higher attrition.
- Employees around **34 years of age** show comparatively higher attrition in the analyzed data.
- **172 employees** are classified as High Risk.
- **744 employees** are classified as Medium Risk.
- **501 employees** are classified as Low Risk.
- Medium Risk employees represent approximately **52.51%** of the workforce.
- **Operations** has the highest number of high-risk employees.
- **Sales Executive** is among the job roles with a high number of high-risk employees.


## 🧮 Flight Risk Scoring

The Flight Risk Score is used to classify employees into different risk categories.

Employees are evaluated using relevant HR factors available in the dataset, such as:

- Overtime
- Job Satisfaction
- Salary
- Employee experience
- Other employee-related attributes

Based on the calculated score, employees are grouped into:

| Risk Level | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| High Risk | Employees who may require immediate retention attention |
| Medium Risk | Employees who may require monitoring and engagement |
| Low Risk | Employees with comparatively lower flight risk |

The score is intended as an **HR decision-support indicator**, not as a guarantee that an employee will leave the organization.
